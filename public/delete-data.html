User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 2026

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to sarthitransport726@gmail.com.
  2. Send it with the subject Delete my Apna Transport data and write the registered phone number of your account in the message.
  3. We confirm that the request is yours (we may ask you to reply from, or confirm, the registered phone number), then delete your data.

Timeline

Your data is deleted within 7 days of a verified request. We will confirm to you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, phone number, address, email).
  • For drivers: KYC documents, photos and vehicle details.
  • Saved location, device and notification identifiers.
  • Your account, so you can no longer log in.

What we may keep

Records we must keep by law or for accounts and tax (for example completed trip and payment records) may be kept for the legally required period, with personal details limited as far as possible.
