If it still doesn't move
  1. Samsung: Settings, Accessibility, Visibility enhancements, turn off Remove animations.
  2. Samsung: turn off Power saving mode (it can switch animations off).
  3. Other Android: Settings, Accessibility, turn off Remove animations; and in Developer options check that Window, Transition and Animator duration scale are not "off".
  4. Close the app fully and reopen it, or tap the orange refresh button.
